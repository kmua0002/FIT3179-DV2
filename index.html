From 151 to 1000+

How the Pokémon universe evolved across generations

Pokémon has expanded dramatically since Generation I. This visualisation explores how the Pokédex, types, statistics and global reach of the franchise have changed over time.

1. The Pokédex kept growing

Every generation has introduced new Pokémon, but the size of each expansion has varied considerably. Generation V introduced 156 Pokémon, slightly more than the original 151 of Generation I.
