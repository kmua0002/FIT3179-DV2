Evolution Across Generations

Generation IV expanded older Pokémon families more than any other generation

Pokémon generations did not simply introduce completely new evolutionary families. Across the nine-generation Pokédex, 71 new species joined families that had first appeared in earlier generations, extending 57 existing families. Generation IV contributed the most, adding 29 species to older families, equivalent to 27.1% of its 107 newly introduced Pokémon. Generations V and VII are absent on the right because neither introduced a species into an older family in this dataset.

Base Statistics

Typical base stats have generally increased in later generations

Pokémon introduced in later generations tend to have higher total base stats, although there is substantial overlap. The median rises from 405 in Generation I to 488.5 in Generation IX.

Total base stats are calculated as the sum of HP, Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense and Speed. Outlying points represent Pokémon beyond 1.5 times the interquartile range.

03
Games & Global Reach

The games evolved alongside Pokémon

As Pokémon expanded across generations, its mainline RPG adventures moved between successive Nintendo consoles. Alongside new releases, remakes revisited older adventures, while international release delays gradually shortened. Together, these changes transformed how Pokémon games were developed, revisited and experienced around the world.

Game & Console Evolution

Pokémon's mainline games crossed six Nintendo hardware platforms

From the Game Boy's Red and Green in 1996 to Legends: Z-A in 2025, Pokémon's core RPG series continued to evolve across Nintendo hardware. However, new games were not limited to introducing new generations. Enhanced versions, sequels, remakes and Legends adventures also revisited and expanded the mainline experience. 04
Pokémon in the Real World

Pokémon stepped into the real world

Beyond video games, Pokémon has become part of Japan's public spaces through Poké Lids, specially designed utility-hole covers featuring Pokémon. This dataset records 470 installations across 41 Japanese prefectures.

Poké Lid Locations

470 Poké Lids reveal an uneven footprint across Japan

Each blue dot represents a recorded Poké Lid. The installations extend across Japan, but some areas have considerably more than others. Hover over a point to discover its location and featured Pokémon.

How to read: Each point represents one Poké Lid. Multiple nearby locations may appear clustered. The map reflects the 470 records in the dataset snapshot, rather than necessarily every Poké Lid currently installed in Japan.

Prefecture Comparison

Four prefectures account for over a third of the recorded Poké Lids

The 470 Poké Lids are not evenly distributed across Japan. Hokkaido leads with 50 recorded installations, followed by Fukushima (43), Miyagi (37) and Iwate (36). Together, these four prefectures account for 35.3% of the dataset.

How to read: Each circle represents one prefecture, with its area proportional to the number of recorded Poké Lids. Circles are positioned at a representative recorded Poké Lid location within each prefecture. Prefectures without records in this dataset are not shown with a circle.

Generation I Footprint

The original 151 feature on two-thirds of recorded Poké Lids

Despite making up only 14.7% of the nine-generation Pokédex, Generation I Pokémon feature on 315 of the 470 recorded Poké Lids (67.0%). Their representation varies considerably across Japan. All 50 recorded Poké Lids in Hokkaido feature at least one Generation I Pokémon, compared with just 4 of 31 in Mie Prefecture.

How to read: Darker prefectures have a higher percentage of recorded Poké Lids featuring at least one Generation I Pokémon. Each lid counts once, even if it features multiple Generation I Pokémon. Grey prefectures have no records in this dataset snapshot, while pale blue can represent a genuine 0%. Percentages are based on the 470-record snapshot, not every Poké Lid currently installed in Japan.

The Bigger Picture

From 151 Pokémon to a worldwide phenomenon

Across nine generations, the Pokédex expanded to 1,025 species, bringing new types and changing battle statistics. Meanwhile, international game releases became more closely coordinated. Yet the original Pokémon remain prominent. In our Japanese Poké Lid dataset, 315 of 470 recorded installations feature at least one Generation I Pokémon. The Pokémon universe has continued to evolve, while its original characters remain a visible part of the franchise's identity.
